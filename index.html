Who We Are

NIS Child Development Centre

A parent NGO and umbrella child-development organisation working to promote the holistic development, protection, education, health, empowerment and well-being of children, families, young people and communities.

Our organisational model

NIS Child Development Centre is the parent NGO, while Nipe International School (Child Development Centre) is its dedicated educational institution.
